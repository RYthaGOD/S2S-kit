# S2S Marketing Playbook

You're not selling a paywall — you're shipping a primitive: **route your staking yield
to anything you value, keep your principal.** Subscriptions are just the first app.

## 🎯 The Hook: "Spend your yield, not your money"
**The Narrative:** "You already hold LSTs earning yield you ignore. S2S lets you point
that yield at the apps, creators, DAOs, or causes you care about — non-custodially, your
principal untouched, reversible any time. A standing order funded by staking rewards."

## 🧭 Use cases (lead with the primitive, not the paywall)
- **Apps** — subscriptions / gated access funded by yield.
- **Creators** — patronage: "Patreon, but you keep your capital."
- **DAOs** — membership dues and treasury contributions from members' yield.
- **Public goods** — *donate your yield, keep your principal* (a strong grant-funding story).

## 💎 Value Propositions
1. **CAC near zero:** users "pay" with opportunity cost (forgone yield), not capital, so the friction of a paywall disappears.
2. **Revenue scales with TVL:** the more LST deposited against your dApp, the larger the yield stream into your treasury.
3. **Works with any LST:** jitoSOL, mSOL, bSOL, JupSOL and more via a config-driven allowlist — no dependency on a single token or an external staking program.

## 💰 The economics (be precise)
- The protocol takes a **small fee** (default 4%) of the *harvested yield only* — never principal.
- The dApp receives the remaining ~96% of the yield.
- Users always withdraw their full original principal value.
- Honest framing beats inflated numbers: don't claim "95% of yield" as guaranteed — actual APR depends on the LST's staking rate.

## 📣 Channel Strategy
- **X (Twitter):** thread on "subscriptions paid by staking yield" with the runnable demo ledger as proof.
- **Solana LST communities** (Jito, Marinade, Sanctum): integration target audiences.
- **Developer advocacy:** position `npx @s2s-kit/cli` as the setup tool.

## 🎨 Aesthetic Direction
- **Name:** S2S (Stake-to-Subscribe).
- **Vibe:** clean, dark-mode, professional, minimalist.

---

You don't compete with Stripe — you make the paywall optional for anyone holding an LST.
