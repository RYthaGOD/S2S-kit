# S2S Marketing Playbook

You aren't just selling a boilerplate; you're selling a new economic paradigm for the mobile web.

## 🎯 The Hook: "Zero-Cost Subscriptions"
**The Narrative:** "Traditional app stores take 30% and force users to pull out a credit card. S2S lets users subscribe for free by putting their money to work. You keep 95% of the yield. They keep 100% of their principal."

## 💎 Value Propositions
1. **CAC Annihilation:** Your Cost of Acquisition (CAC) drops to near-zero because the user "pays" with opportunity cost, not capital.
2. **Infinite Runway:** As your TVL (Total Value Locked) grows, your revenue becomes a self-sustaining, inflationary-protected treasury.
3. **Hardware Sovereignty:** Built specifically for the Solana Seeker. No gatekeepers. No middleman (except for a tiny 5% infrastructure fee).

## 📣 Channel Strategy
- **X (Twitter):** Run a thread on the benefits of stake-based monetization.
- **Solana Mobile Ecosystem:** Pitch as a native monetization standard for Seeker dApps.
- **Developer Advocacy:** Position `npx @s2s-kit/cli init` as the standard setup tool.

## 🎨 Aesthetic Direction
- **Name:** S2S (Stake-to-Subscribe).
- **Vibe:** Clean, dark-mode, professional.
- **Style:** Minimalist and device-native.

---

This is how you win. You don't compete with Stripe; you make Stripe irrelevant for the Seeker economy.
